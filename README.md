# Business Profitability Diagnostic

## Project Overview

**Business Profitability Diagnostic** is a web-based application designed to help small business owners and independent professionals better understand their financial performance and identify potential profitability issues.

Many small businesses track revenue but struggle to determine how much they actually keep after expenses, how much cash is tied up in overdue payments, and how dependent they are on a single customer. This project aims to make these business indicators easier to understand and act upon.

## The Business Problem

Small businesses often face financial challenges because they lack a simple way to evaluate their profitability, outstanding receivables, customer concentration, and use of available working hours.

Without a clear financial overview, business owners may find it difficult to identify risks and prioritize the actions that could improve their operations.

## The Solution

The application provides a guided financial diagnostic based on the user's most recent monthly figures. Its purpose is to translate business data into understandable indicators and help users identify areas that may require attention.

The landing page introduces the diagnostic process, explains its benefits, and guides users toward the assessment.

## Key Features

- **Profit Margin Analysis:** Understand how much remains after business costs and expenses.
- **Overdue Receivables:** Assess the money tied up in late customer payments.
- **Customer Concentration:** Consider the financial risk of depending heavily on one customer.
- **Billable Hours Analysis:** Understand how much of the available working time is actually billed.
- **Financial Health Assessment:** Review the diagnostic results and identify areas for further investigation.
- **Action-Oriented Guidance:** Help users consider potential solutions and appropriate next steps.

## User Journey

1. **Enter Monthly Figures** — Provide information about revenue, costs, overdue payments, and working hours.
2. **Review the Diagnostic** — Examine profitability indicators and the financial assessment.
3. **Identify Priorities** — Review suggested solutions and consider the next steps for the business.

## Technology Stack

- **HTML:** Page structure and content.
- **CSS:** Responsive layout, visual styling, and light/dark theme support.

## Business Value

The project focuses on making financial analysis more accessible to small businesses and independent professionals. By organizing key indicators into a guided experience, it aims to help users move from raw monthly figures toward better-informed business decisions.

This project demonstrates the development of a web-based financial diagnostic experience, combining user-centered interface design with business-focused financial indicators.
